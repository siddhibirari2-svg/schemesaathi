Government Scheme Fraud Shield

Essential rules to protect yourself against scheme scams, fake portals, and phishing.

What Government Portals NEVER Ask

  • Never share Aadhaar OTP: Government portals do not call you to ask for OTP.
  • Never share Bank / UPI PIN: Government schemes credit money into your account; they NEVER require PIN entry.
  • Never pay cash to unauthorized agents: Most scheme registrations on official portals are 100% free.
  • Never share portal passwords: Keep your DigiLocker & e-District logins private.

How to Spot Official Sources (.gov.in / .nic.in)

  • Check the domain extension: Official Government websites strictly end with .gov.in or .nic.in.
  • Beware of fake .com/.org clones: Fake sites often use names like pm-kisan-yojana.com.
  • Look for HTTPS lock: Ensure connection security on all government pages.
  • Use SchemeSaathi verified links: Every link on SchemeSaathi is audited against the official registry.
